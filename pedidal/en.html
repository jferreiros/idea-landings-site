Before you hand us your orders.

Do customers have to change anything?

No. They write to the same WhatsApp number. The only new thing is an order summary to confirm with "ok".

What if the AI picks the wrong product?

Each line has a confidence score. When unsure, Pedidal sends the line to the review inbox or asks the customer. The customer also confirms the summary before anything reaches the ERP.

Does it work with my ERP?

We start with A3, Sage, Holded and Odoo. For other ERPs we start with a CSV or email in the format the ERP already imports, so a pilot starts in days.

Is the official WhatsApp Business API required?

Yes. We use Meta's official API and help migrate the current number or register a new one. Customers notice no difference.

What does the pilot cost?

Nothing. No card needed. If the 20 orders do not convince, we stop there. Otherwise it is month to month.

How long until it runs?

Pilot: 48 hours. CSV or email export: first week. A direct ERP connector depends on the ERP, and we say how long on the first call.

We already have a B2B portal. Why add this?

If customers already use the portal, skip it. Pedidal handles the orders that still arrive on WhatsApp, which is usually most of them.

You are new. What if you disappear?

Orders always end up in the distributor's own ERP, and the WhatsApp number stays theirs. On exit they keep the catalogue, learned aliases and history in Excel.

What happens to customer data?

We act as a GDPR data processor with a signed agreement and EU hosting. Orders are never used to train third-party models.
