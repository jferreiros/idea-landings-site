The manager's contractors, the manager's rules

Urgency rules set by the office

Active leaks, people stuck in a lift, no light in common areas. These go straight to the on-call contractor, at night or on Sundays.

Spend the office approves

Above a set amount, Conserjia requests a quote and asks. It never promises a resident who will pay.

Shared or private, by the law

It knows the basics of the Spanish Horizontal Property Act. Doubtful cases go to the manager.

Residents install nothing

They message a WhatsApp number on a sign in the entrance hall, in Spanish or Catalan.

Answering is not closing

A chatbot that answers questions helps. A repair is done when the contractor has been and the resident confirms.

Before the sign goes up in the hall

Does it replace the office team?

No. It removes the errands: asking for the address, calling the plumber, chasing the visit, updating the resident. The team decides doubtful cases and approves spend.

What if the contractor does not answer?

Conserjia sends a reminder. After the office's deadline, it moves to the next contractor on the list or alerts the office.

What if the AI gets urgency wrong?

Urgency follows rules the office sets. In doubtful cases Conserjia asks before dispatching.

Does the office change software?

No. Conserjia runs alongside from day one. Integration with Netfincas and CIAX, the common Spanish tools, is on the roadmap.

What about residents' data?

Conserjia acts as data processor for the office under a GDPR contract, keeps only what it needs and stores it in the EU.

What if the fault is private?

Conserjia explains it to the resident, suggests their home insurer and logs it. It does not send a contractor at the building's cost.

Do residents know it is an AI?

Yes. It says so in the first message, as the EU AI Act requires.
