What garages ask us

Will customers know it is an AI?

Yes, on purpose. Motaller introduces itself as the garage assistant. If a customer asks for a person, it hands over at once.

Do I need a new number?

No. We connect your WhatsApp Business number. You can still write from the garage phone.

What if it quotes a wrong price?

It only gives the base prices you set. Any diagnostic quote comes from you: you take the photos and confirm the price before it goes out.

Does it work with rider fleets?

Yes. Mark a bike as "rider" and it gets priority with a delivery time. A fleet can have its own account.

What about customer data?

It belongs to the garage. EU servers, a GDPR processor agreement, and export any time.

I have no time for setup.

We set it up in one afternoon at the garage. No tutorials, no change to your invoicing software.
