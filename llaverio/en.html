Three steps. Your team keeps its apps.

01

Connect your PMS

Llaverio reads check-ins and check-outs from Avantio, Hostaway, Smoobu or an iCal feed. You add your flats, cleaners and technicians.

02

The agent coordinates

After each check-out it messages the cleaner on WhatsApp, asks for photos and checks them against the flat’s list. If it spots a fault, it opens a job and proposes a visit.

03

You decide what matters

It only writes when it needs your OK: a cost, a serious complaint or a schedule change. At month end it drafts each owner report.

Before you hand us the keys.

How much work is setup?

One hour on a call. You give read access to your PMS and the list of flats, cleaners and technicians. We build each flat’s checklist from your photos and have it running within a week.

Does my cleaning team install anything?

No. Llaverio writes through WhatsApp Business from your company number. Cleaners reply and send photos as they do today.

Does it replace Chekin, Avantio or my PMS?

No. Your PMS stays the source of bookings and Chekin (or similar) keeps handling guest registration for SES.Hospedajes. Llaverio covers what happens between one check-out and the next check-in.

What if the agent gets it wrong?

You set what it decides alone and what needs your OK. Costs, serious complaints and schedule changes always go through you. Every action is logged.

What about guest and staff data?

We act as data processor under GDPR, with servers in the EU, and use only what we need to coordinate the work.

What about winter, when half my flats are empty?

You pay per active flat: one with at least one check-out that month. 12 active flats in January means you pay for 12. There is a 60 € monthly minimum per manager. From November to March you can switch to the Winter plan: 6 € per active flat, 30 € minimum.

Will my cleaners accept it?

They get the same WhatsApp as today, minus the midnight voice notes and unannounced changes. Before we start, we ask for their consent and explain the photos: the flat only, never people.
